# Synopsis (from the extracted script, phase 6)

For translators and the phase 9 story pass. Sub-table order is story order; most events exist in three copies, one per
chosen hero (Glen / Kageri / Gant), with the other two as supporting speakers.

## Cast
- **Glen** (Shota): hot-headed hero. **Gant** (Naoya): calm, the strategist; Kageri calls him Naoya-san.
  **Kageri** (Yuka): brave, teasing. Together the Justirisers, given power by Norn's Justi-Power.
- **Doctor Zora**: Kaiser Hades' scientist, plotting Hades' revival; escapes every time until the finale.
- Generals: **Zauras** (stage 1, revived in stage 5 speaking in broken katakana), **Gildoros** (stage 2, "Gigiii!"),
  **Armor Gunner** (stage 3), **Gardias** (stage 3-4, mind-controls one hero). Grunts: **Zakoul**; robots: Bugscenes.
- Giant forms: the Justirisers summon the Genseishin **Raizeros** (s22 幻星神, s35 げんせいじゅう) and **Kenriser**.

## Chapters
1. **Prologue** (s07): Sengoku-era sky battle; Norn vs Kaiser Hades, both fall to Earth; Zora resumes the invasion;
   Norn's Justi-Power awakens the Justirisers.
2. **Stage 1** (s08-s11): Zauras attacks the city; roll calls ("Souchaku!"); the city burns while Zakoul stall the
   heroes; Zauras goes giant; Zora's voice: Hades' revival is near. Narration closes the chapter.
3. **Stage 2** (s13-s16): Zakoul lead the heroes to an old factory; Gildoros guards it; Zora appears, leaves Gildoros
   to fight and escapes with what was built; Naoya planted a tracker on Zora.
4. **Stage 3** (s18-s25): chasing Zora; Armor Gunner ambushes; a Zakoul truck carries missiles; Gardias ambushes the
   group and one hero goes missing; the others split up to search.
5. **Stage 4** (s26): the missing hero is under Gardias' control and attacks; freed; a hidden passage; Genseishin
   attacks fail against a new enemy.
6. **Stage 5** (s28-s32): a cave; revived Zauras, Gildoros, Armor Gunner; Zora's base: missiles launch in one hour;
   smashing the control unit triggers self-destruct; the heroes destroy the main computer; Zora summons Rhinoslave,
   then the giant Troidon; Zora retreats; ending narration: the fight against Kaiser Hades goes on.
- s35: battle shouts (summons, finishers, damage cries). s00-s06: menus, save, missions, stage list.
